The words hung in the air. I could see them hanging in his breath, fogging in the iciness. A train rumbles past in the tracks behind him.
My answer materializes, I can see the words flashing in my mind, but I can't bring myself to say even one of them. "Do you want a cigarette?"
His eyelashes flutter slightly, his eyes clear, dark, direct. He has strong eyebrows, in a way that make his eyes look soft. I look away, rummage in my pockets for my pack and a lighter.
I light the lighter, but the Boston winds are brutal. The flame is barely there. I try pressing my cigarette into where the flame should be.
"Here." He cups his hands, trying to shield the wind, but then I don't light the lighter. His hands are too close. Easy to burn.
"Try it again," he says.
"No, it's okay." I put the cigarette back into the pack.
